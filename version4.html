About

Ancient Trees, Living Landscapes

Mapping Israel’s Heritage Orchards

Ancient orchards and heritage fruit trees are living witnesses to the agricultural and cultural history of the Eastern Mediterranean. Many of these landscapes are rapidly disappearing due to urban development, climate change, and the loss of traditional farming systems.

The BOSTAN TREE project combines archaeology, genetics, history, and GIS mapping to document and preserve heritage orchards and landrace fruit trees across Israel. Through interactive maps and spatial databases, visitors can explore the distribution of heritage trees, historical orchards, and the agricultural landscapes that shaped them.

Many of these heritage varieties show remarkable resilience to drought and environmental stress, making them important resources for climate adaptation and the future of sustainable agriculture.

This platform serves as a gateway to the evolving landscape of heritage orchard research and preservation.
